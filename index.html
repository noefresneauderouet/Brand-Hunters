          New game

            How to play

            Rules

            Describe it. Draw it. Hunt it down!

            The world’s most famous brands have escaped. Listen to the Witness, find the Fugitive and collect the bounty!

            It’s a learning game too: the Witness practises describing in English, the Hunter practises listening, and everyone discovers the story behind each logo.

            • 2 to 4 players
            • 54 cards (50 Fugitives + 4 Jokers) and a score sheet (QR code on the box)
            • You need paper, pencils and a timer

            1Goal

            Collect the highest total bounty by identifying brand logos.

            2Roles

            • Hunter: names the brand and can draw the logo.
            • Witness: sees the card and describes the logo.
            • Vultures: all the other players. They wait for a mistake to ambush the Hunter.

            3Setup

            • Sort the cards into 4 piles: , , and . Each pile has one Joker. Shuffle, face down.
            • Choose the number of rounds. In one round, every player is the Hunter once.
            • The youngest player is the first Hunter. The player on their left is the Witness.

            4Card levels

            • Pickpocket $100 45 s3 forbidden words
            • Bandit $200 45 s3 forbidden words
            • Outlaw $300 60 s3 forbidden words
            • Most Wanted $500 75 sno forbidden words

            Missed shot: −$50 at every level.

            • Jokers unlock bonuses. When the Witness draws one, they show it, keep it and draw another card from the same pile. Each Joker is used once, only when its owner is the Hunter. Follow the card.

            In the app, tap the time on The Hunt to set 45, 60 or 75 s.

            5Playing a turn

            1. The Hunter chooses a pile.
            2. The Witness takes the top card, keeps it secret and starts the timer.
            3. The Witness describes the logo in English.
            4. The Hunter can draw the logo (it doesn’t need to be perfect) and must name the brand.
            5. The turn ends when the brand is found, the bounty reaches $0, or time runs out.
            6. The Witness shows the card and reads the logo’s story aloud.
            7. The next player on the left becomes the Hunter.

            6The Witness’s rules

            Allowed

            Shapes, colours, positions and other visual hints.

            Not allowed

            The brand name or any translation of it, its slogan, its products, forbidden words, gestures, or any language except English.

            Breaking a rule counts as a missed shot, but doesn’t allow an ambush.

            7Missed shots & Ambush!

            • Each wrong name is a missed shot: the bounty drops by $50. At $0, the turn ends.
            • A player’s score can never decrease.
            • After a wrong name, the timer pauses. The first Vulture to call “Ambush!” gets one guess:
              • Correct: that Vulture wins half of the card’s original bounty, and the turn ends.
              • Wrong: no penalty, but that Vulture can’t ambush again this turn. The timer resumes.
            • If a wrong name brings the bounty to $0, an ambush is still allowed before the turn ends.
            • With 2 players, there are no Vultures, so no ambush.

            8Scoring

            • Hunter: the bounty left after capturing the Fugitive.
            • Witness: half of that bounty (nothing after an ambush).
            • Vulture: half of the card’s original bounty after a successful ambush.
            • Quick capture: +$50 for the Hunter who guesses right in under 15 seconds.
            • Collection: whoever finds the logo keeps the card. At the end, every 3 cards from the same gang earn +$100.
              • Social & Streaming
              • Tech & Gaming
              • Sport & Fashion
              • Cars
              • Food & Business

            In the app: Wrong = missed shot, Bonus = quick capture. Tap a hunter in the list to pay the Witness or a Vulture, and use Custom for any other amount.

            9Final round

            All bounties and bonuses earned in the final round are doubled, except collection bonuses.

            10Winning

            • The highest total is the “Best Hunter in Town”.
            • Tie: the player with the most captured cards wins. Still tied: the tied players play another round.

            Have fun, Brand Hunters!
